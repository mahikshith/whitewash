# Design critique and refresh plan

4 November 2026. Covers the live Phase 4 site (commit `fe67ebe`): homepage, the eight format pages, Convert, the text editor and the single-tool pages, in light, dark and at phone width. Screenshots are in [`screenshots/2026-11-critique/`](screenshots/2026-11-critique/).

## Verdict

Don't redesign it. The bones are good: the dial is distinctive, the warm paper and lime palette doesn't look like every other Tailwind SaaS template, and the type pairing (Bricolage headings, system body) is right. What's missing is the stuff that makes a stranger trust it enough to pay. Right now it reads as a nicely made free utility. Nothing on the page sells the one thing worth paying for (Humanize on real documents), nothing proves the privacy claim, and the phone layout hides the navigation.

So the plan is a refresh in three phases, not a rebuild. Phase A is in this PR.

## What works (keep it)

- **The dial.** Nobody else in the PDF or humanizer space has a signature interaction. iLovePDF and Smallpdf are tile grids; QuillBot, Undetectable and friends are two textareas. The dial is memorable and it really is one click after a drop. Keep it big.
- **Format tabs with mega-menus.** The PDF drop-down (Popular, From PDF, To PDF, Pages, Secure, Fix) is as good as iLovePDF's and better grouped.
- **The editor.** Side by side, every edit highlighted, "See changes" to undo one by one. That's the paid-tier feature set already, and it's better than most humanizers, which show you a rewritten blob with no diff.
- **Palette and dark mode.** Both themes hold up. The lime is used sparingly enough to mean "do this".
- **Honest notes.** The "You get / Keeps / Drops" card beside each conversion is the best trust device on the site. Competitors don't tell you what a conversion loses.

## What's wrong

Ordered by how much it costs you.

1. **The homepage doesn't say what the product is.** "Drop a file. Pick one thing. Done." could be any converter. The paid feature, humanizing whole documents and getting the same file back, was buried in a lede sentence between "convert anything" and "shrink PDFs". A visitor from a "humanize AI text" search can't tell they're in the right place. *(Fixed in Phase A.)*
2. **No proof that Humanize works.** Every humanizer competitor puts a before/after on the first screen. Ours showed a three-step "How it works" card that repeated the headline. *(Fixed: the card is now a real before/after, generated by the engine, that swaps to live info when you point at the dial.)*
3. **The privacy claim is a slogan, not evidence.** "Files never leave your device" sat in an eyebrow in tiny caps. In 2026 that line is everywhere: a whole crop of "iLovePDF alternative that doesn't upload your files" sites use it. It needs specifics to be believed. *(Phase A adds a three-part trust row: never uploaded, no account, no tracking. All three are true: the CSP only allows same-origin connections and there are no analytics.)* Phase B should go further (see below).
4. **Phone navigation was broken in practice.** At 390 px the tab row clipped to "Humanize" with no scroll hint, so PDF, Word, Excel and the rest were unreachable without the search button. *(Fixed: tabs get their own scrolling row with a fade on the edge.)* The dial's centre also lost its "Drop a file" label at that size. *(Fixed.)*
5. **Format pages said everything twice.** On `#pdf` the lede and the info card printed the identical sentence. *(Fixed: the idle card now lists the jobs on the dial and the one useful tip, "drop a file first and the dial only shows what fits".)*
6. **Single-tool pages are two-step forms.** Compress: a big "Choose a PDF" button, then a greyed "Compress PDF" button underneath it. That's exactly the iLovePDF pattern mykee said to beat. The grey disabled button reads as broken. Phase A makes the entry a visible drop zone. Phase B should run the job as soon as the file lands, using the default setting, and let people change the setting and re-run.
7. **No pricing or upgrade surface at all.** There's nowhere for money to happen and nothing that hints a paid tier exists. See Phase C.
8. **"← All tools" goes to the homepage, which no longer lists tools.** From Compress, the back link should go to PDF.
9. **The footer was one grey sentence.** No links, no source, nothing to explore. *(Fixed: brand, a short privacy line, section links, Source on GitHub, Privacy, Help.)*
10. **Smaller things.** The empty editor told phone users to look "on the left". *(Fixed.)* Format-page tool cards leave ragged rows (3 cards in a 4-column grid). The dial's outer disc has a heavy grey "bowl" at the bottom in light mode that makes it look like it's sinking. Convert's "From" panel cuts off "Video (sound only)" with no scroll cue.

## A functional bug that hurts the pitch

Humanizing "In today's fast-paced world, it is important to note that teamwork is crucial." returns "teamwork is crucial." The first letter isn't re-capitalised when two stock phrases are cut back to back. A single phrase is handled; two in a row isn't. This is the first thing a paying user would see go wrong, so it should be fixed before any pricing goes live. (Flagged for the functional-testing work rather than fixed here.)

## What competitors do that we should borrow

| Who | What they do well | What we take |
|---|---|---|
| iLovePDF, Smallpdf | One tool per URL, file picker is the whole first screen, the job runs on drop. Clear free limits ("2 tasks a day") shown at the moment you hit them. Pro removes limits and adds batch. | Run on drop (Phase B). Put limits in the moment, not in Help. |
| Smallpdf | Trust strip under the tool: security certifications, "files deleted after 1 hour", user counts. | Our version is stronger because nothing is uploaded at all. Show it as evidence, not a promise (Phase B). |
| QuillBot, Grammarly | Before/after demo on the landing page, free word cap with an inline "upgrade for more" when you hit it, modes behind a lock icon. | Before/after card (done). A locked "Rewrite in your voice" mode beside Humanize when the LLM tier exists. |
| Undetectable, Humbot, StealthGPT | Word-count meters and big pricing tables. Lots of "bypass detectors" claims. | Take the meter, skip the claims. We can't promise detector bypass and shouldn't try; "reads like you wrote it" is the honest pitch. |
| Tangerine (Mac) | The radial wheel as the whole UI. | Already ours. |

The opening we have that they don't: **document humanizing that returns the same Word, PowerPoint or Excel file**, with styles intact, without uploading it. Every humanizer competitor is paste-text-only, and every PDF suite that does AI uploads your file. Lead with that.

## Phase A (this PR)

- Homepage headline and lede now name the product: "Take the AI out of any document."
- Homepage info card is a before/after for Humanize (real engine output).
- Trust row: never uploaded, no account, no tracking.
- Format pages: no duplicate text in the info card.
- Phone: tabs on their own scrolling row; dial centre label fits.
- Single-tool pages: the entry looks like a drop zone.
- Footer with links and source.
- Editor empty state copy no longer says "on the left".

No layout, dial, or flow changes. Everything mykee fixed in Phase 4 (dial size, plain + centre, no badges, one Humanize button) is untouched.

## Phase B (next, no decision needed)

1. **Run on drop.** Single-tool pages start the job as soon as the file arrives with the default setting, then show the result with "Change setting and redo". Kill the disabled grey button.
2. **Proof of privacy.** A small "Check it yourself" link that opens a note: turn on airplane mode after the page loads and the core tools still work; the page's security policy blocks outside connections. Add a live "0 bytes uploaded" counter in the status pill after each job.
3. **Back links go to the format page** the tool lives on.
4. **Fix the capitalisation bug** above.
5. **Lighten the dial's bowl** in light mode and even out the card grid.
6. **Phone dial first.** On phones, put the dial right under the headline and the before/after card below it.

## Phase C (needs mykee's call): the paid surface

Nothing is built for this yet, because what to charge for changes the product. My recommendation:

- **Free forever:** everything that exists today. Converters, PDF tools and rule-based Humanize stay free, no limits. That's the funnel and the reason people bookmark it; capping it now would just push them back to iLovePDF.
- **Pro (around $8 a month or $60 a year):** the things that cost us money or are clearly "work" features:
  - "Rewrite in your voice", the LLM rewrite already parked in `research/later.md`, with a monthly word allowance.
  - Batch Humanize (drop 50 Word files, get a ZIP back).
  - Saved "never change these phrases" lists and a personal style profile.
- **Where it shows up:** a "Pro" item at the right of the header; a locked third segment on the document dial ("Rewrite") that opens a short upsell instead of a pricing table; a `#pricing` page with two columns, not four.

Smallpdf and iLovePDF both price around $7 to $15 a month for Pro, and humanizer tools sit in the same band, so $8 is in range. Treat that number as a placeholder until there's an LLM cost per word to price against.
